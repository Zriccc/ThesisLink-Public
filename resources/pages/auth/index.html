ThesisLink Terms and Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Terms of Use

ThesisLink is the CCSICT research repository of Isabela State University–Cabagan. Use your account for legitimate academic and repository activities.

  • Provide accurate account and research information.
  • Upload only work and documents you are authorized to submit.
  • Do not misuse accounts, disrupt the service, or upload unlawful or harmful material.
  • Published records may be reviewed, corrected, restricted, or removed by authorized CCSICT personnel.

Privacy Policy

ThesisLink processes your name, email, account role, repository activity, and submitted research information to operate and secure the repository. Authentication is handled through Supabase, while uploaded thesis and approval files are stored in private Cloudflare R2 storage.

Approved thesis information and permitted documents may be made available through the repository. Access to administrative information is limited by account role. Contact the ThesisLink administrator or CCSICT office to request correction of your account information or ask about removal and retention.

By registering, you confirm that you understand these terms and consent to this handling of your information.
